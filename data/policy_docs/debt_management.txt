Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio Guidelines
A healthy DTI ratio is below 36%. Between 36% and 50% is considered moderate risk, and above 50% indicates high financial risk. To reduce DTI, prioritize paying off high-interest debt first (avalanche method) or smallest balances first (snowball method).

EMI Management
Your total EMIs should not exceed 40% of your monthly income. If EMIs exceed this, consider consolidating loans or refinancing at lower interest rates. Avoid taking new loans while existing EMIs are high.

Emergency Fund
Maintain at least 3 to 6 months of living expenses as an emergency fund. This protects you from unexpected expenses without resorting to high-interest credit.
