Credit Utilization Best Practices
Keep your credit utilization below 30% of your total credit limit. Below 10% is ideal. High utilization (above 50%) significantly increases financial risk and negatively affects credit score.

Reducing Credit Utilization
Pay down balances before statement dates. Request a credit limit increase (without spending more). Distribute spending across multiple cards to lower per-card utilization.

Credit Card Repayment
Always pay at least the full statement balance to avoid interest. Minimum payments lead to long-term debt accumulation.
